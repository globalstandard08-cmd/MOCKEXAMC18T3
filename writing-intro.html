WRITING TEST

Time allowed: 60 minutes

Number of tasks: 2

Instructions

  • The Writing test consists of two tasks.
  • Task 1: You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Write at least 150 words.
  • Task 2: You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Write at least 250 words.
  • Both tasks are weighted equally in marking.
  • You may plan your answers in the space provided, but you will receive no marks for notes.
  • Write clearly — you may alter your answers as you go.

Click the button below when you are ready to begin.

Once you begin, you may move freely between Task 1 and Task 2.
